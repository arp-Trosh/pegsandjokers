The root cause is architectural: `Layout._build()` in `game/board.py` draws a literal, trigonometrically-accurate regular polygon (rectangle/hexagon/octagon) with each side fixed at `ARM_LEN=18` holes. Since side length is fixed, the whole polygon's diameter scales up with the number of slots — an octagon for 8 players is roughly twice the diameter of the 4-player rectangle, and the diagonal arms of the hexagon/octagon get an extra `perp_boost` multiplier (up to ~5x) on the HOME/SAFE cluster offsets just to keep them visually separated after rounding to character cells. So more players doesn't just mean "more crowded," it means a genuinely bigger shape — that's why `BoardView` currently punts on the problem entirely by making it a `ScrollView` and letting the player pan around a canvas that can be larger than the terminal.

A geometry change can fix this, but it means giving up literal polygon fidelity in favor of a schematic layout. The option I'd reach for first: instead of a radial ring, render each player's arm as its own fixed-width horizontal lane, stacked vertically (like a scoreboard) — think "unrolled" board rather than "ring-shaped" board. That changes the scaling law from radial (diameter grows with slot count) to linear (one more row per player), which is the only approach that can plausibly get 8 players to fit with zero or near-zero scrolling in a normal terminal — a few dozen rows tall is much more common than a few dozen columns wide once player count grows. It also sidesteps the diagonal-arm `perp_boost` problem entirely since every lane is axis-aligned.

Complementary, smaller-lift options if a full re-geometry is more than you want right now: shrink the HOME/SAFE offset distances and rely on color/style instead of spacing to separate them from the track holes; fold each 18-hole arm into two rows of 9 instead of one row of 18 to cut horizontal footprint at the cost of some vertical space; or make the scale constants (`HOLE_UNIT`, `COL_SCALE`) adaptive to player count so 6/8-player boards render smaller than 4-player ones instead of uniformly bigger. None of those alone get you to "no scrolling" at 8 players, but they'd meaningfully shrink the 6-player case and could be combined with the lane-based redesign.

This is worth prototyping properly in the main conversation once it's free — happy to sketch the lane-based `Layout` variant then.
